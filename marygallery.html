01 The Artist

Between Waking
and Dream

Maryam Hany is a surrealist visual artist whose work inhabits the liminal space between consciousness and reverie. Her canvases breathe with otherworldly light — figures dissolving into mist, architectures folding back upon themselves, time suspended in amber and violet.

Drawing from the rich traditions of Arabic mysticism and contemporary dreamcore aesthetics, Maryam constructs worlds that feel achingly familiar yet impossibly distant — as if you have visited them only in the deepest hours of sleep, and spent the waking day trying, desperately, to remember.

"I do not paint what I see.
I paint what I cannot forget."

Her practice moves fluidly between digital media, oil, and mixed-media collage — each piece a controlled surrender to the unconscious. What emerges is never planned. It is uncovered.
